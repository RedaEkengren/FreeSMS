# When FreeSMS is down

For the people at the counter and in the workshop. Print it and keep it where the
job cards are. It does not need the computer.

## First, two minutes

1. Try the page again, and from another device. If only yours fails, it is your
   connection, not the workshop's system.
2. Ring the person named on the support line of your **Getting started** page
   (or in `RUNBOOK.md`, "Who to ring"). Say what you see, not what you think.
3. Carry on with paper. Everything below is for that.

## What to write down on paper

**A car comes in:** the time, the registration, the owner's name and telephone
number, what they say is wrong, what they agreed to, the odometer if you read it.

**Work is done:** the registration, who did it, from when to when, the parts used.

**A part comes or goes:** what, how many, the supplier, the delivery note number.

**Money arrives:** the invoice number it is for, the amount, how (bank, cash, card,
Swish), the date it arrived.

**The car is collected:** the time, who took it, the odometer.

## What not to do

- **Do not give an invoice a number from paper.** Invoice numbers are one
  unbroken series, made by the system, and a hole in it is a document that is
  missing. Write "preliminary" on a sheet and give the customer a note of what
  they owe; the invoice is made when the system is back.
- **Do not promise a customer the system's link, a text or an email** while it is
  down. Say you will send it.
- **Do not start entering things half-way** if the system comes back looking
  strange (jobs missing, a page asking you to set up the workshop). Stop and ring.

## When it is back

Somebody does this once, calmly, before anyone starts entering the paper.

1. **Find out what is missing.** The person who brought it back reads you the
   time of the **newest thing the system has** (the restore says it). Everything
   on paper from after that time is yours to enter; anything from before is
   already there.
2. **Look before you enter.** For each car: search the registration. If the job is
   there, add to it, do not open another. For each payment: open the day (**The
   day**) and look at the money recorded; if it is there, leave it.
3. **Enter in this order:** jobs, then the work and parts on them, then invoices,
   then payments. Give each payment the date it really arrived, and the
   reference from the paper.
4. **Check the invoice numbers.** The last number the system has must be the next
   one after the last invoice you know went out. If a customer holds an invoice
   with a number the system does not have, that is a document lost in the
   outage: tell the owner at once, do not issue another with the same number.
5. **Tick the paper** as entered, with the date, and keep it for the owner.
6. **Tell the owner** what was lost and what was entered. They write it in the
   incident log (`RUNBOOK.md`, "Incident log").

Text and email the system sent before it went down are on the job as sent. If a
customer says they got nothing, check the job's timeline before sending again.
