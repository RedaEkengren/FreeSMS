Personal data

What is kept, and for how long

  • Issued invoicesseven years
    Required by Swedish bookkeeping law, including the name and address they were addressed to. This is why erasure anonymises a person rather than deleting them.
  • A vehicle's technical historykept
    It belongs to the vehicle, not to a person, and survives a sale. The next owner sees what was done and not who paid.
  • Clocked hourskept
    They support payroll records and stay attributable to a person whose name may have been cleared.
  • Failed sign-in attempts90 days
    They exist to slow down guessing over minutes. Keeping them longer would be keeping a record of who tried to sign in, with no remaining purpose.
  • Customer inspection links180 days
    The link itself stops working after a fortnight; the record that one was sent is kept a while longer, then removed.

There is no national identity number and no driving licence field anywhere in this system. A field with no purpose does not exist.
