Questions

What does it cost?

Nothing. FreeSMS is open source under AGPL-3.0. There is no hosted service to pay for: the workshop runs it on its own server, and anybody who wants help with that can hire whoever they like.

Where are our customers’ details kept?

With you. The database is on the workshop’s own server, not ours. And inside the system only the roles that need a customer’s name and phone number see them – the technician’s screen never fetches them.

Does it work with our accounts package, or our accountant?

FreeSMS hands over the month’s invoices and payments as an SIE 4 file – the standard format Swedish bookkeeping programs read, Fortnox and Visma among them. No integration to pay for or wait to be approved. The file is checked against an independent SIE reader on every change to the code; it has not yet been tried against a commercial program, and we would rather say so than pretend. Outside Sweden there is no export yet.

Do we need a certified cash register?

FreeSMS is built for invoiced sales and has no till. In Sweden, invoiced sales are not covered by the certified cash register requirement, but how that applies to your business is a question for your accountant.

What happens when the connection drops?

What is written is kept in the phone and sent, exactly once, when the connection is back. That is the first of the closer scenes on this page, and it runs as a test on every change.

Is it ready to use?

It is early. A car can go all the way from the door to the books, but there are things left – they are open as issues in the source code, with what is missing and why.

Run it on your own server

Docker, one password and nothing else. Behind HTTPS the whole workshop reaches it.

cp .env.example .env
# set POSTGRES_PASSWORD in .env
docker compose up
